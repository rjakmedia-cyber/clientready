Book a 30-min Strategy Call

We'll walk through your ICP, your offer, and your current pipeline. By the end, you'll know whether this would work for your business — and whether you're a fit for the cohort.

What to expect on this call

30 minutes. Founder-led. No generic deck, no junior salesperson. We dig into the specifics of your business and either propose how we'd build pipeline for you, or tell you straight that you're not a fit.

0–5 min · Your business

Quick context: who you sell to, current pipeline sources, what's working, what isn't.

5–15 min · Diagnose

We map your funnel: lead source → response → meeting → close. Identify where it's leaking revenue.

15–25 min · The build

If we're a fit, we walk through what your specific outbound engine would look like. Numbers, timelines, deliverables.

25–30 min · Decision

You'll know if you want to join the cohort. We'll know if we want to take you on. No pressure — clean yes/no.

You're a fit if

  • You're a B2B SaaS, agency, or professional service
  • You're doing $500k+ in annual revenue
  • You're spending $3k+/mo on lead gen, marketing, or SDRs
  • You can take 5+ qualified meetings/week if we deliver them
  • You're the decision-maker (founder, CEO, VP Sales)

You're not a fit if

  • You sell B2C / consumer products
  • Average customer LTV is under $5k
  • You're pre-revenue / pre-product
  • You want a cheap retainer, not a system
