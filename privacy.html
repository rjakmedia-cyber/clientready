Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

This policy explains what clientReady collects on clientready.agency and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, email, phone, company, website, and the onboarding information about your offer and ideal clients.
  • Payment details, which are entered on and processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Basic site analytics, such as pages visited and device type.

How we use it

  • To deliver your plan: building your target list, writing and sending your campaigns, and booking your meetings.
  • To bill you and send receipts through Stripe.
  • To contact you about your account.

Who we share it with

Only the services we need to run your plan, such as Stripe for payments and the tools that deliver our onboarding form and email campaigns. We do not sell your information.

Prospect data

For campaigns, we use business contact information about decision makers at companies. Anyone we email can opt out at any time, and we honor opt-outs within 10 business days.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing us.
