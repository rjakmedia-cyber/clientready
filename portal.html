    Help

    How do I get the most from my meetings?

    Keep open slots on your calendar, join on time, and follow up the same day. Tell us after each call whether it showed and whether it was a fit. That feedback is how we sharpen the list every month.

    A meeting wasn't a fit. What do I do?

    Mark it "Not a fit" in Your meetings within 48 hours and add a short note. We review every one and adjust who we target.

    Can I change who we're reaching or how the emails sound?

    Yes. Send a message above with what you want changed. We'll confirm and show you the update before it goes out.

    When do meetings start?

    Month 1 is building and warming up your sending network. Meetings usually start once sending begins in week 4, ramp in month 2, and your plan's full monthly target starts in month 3.

    Does any of this touch my main email or domain?

    No. Everything sends from a separate network we set up and manage for you. Your everyday email isn't touched.

    How do I pause, change plans or cancel?

    Send a message with "Upgrade or change plan", "Pause" or "Cancel". Plans have a 3-month minimum, then run month to month with 30 days' notice. Upgrades start at your next billing date.
