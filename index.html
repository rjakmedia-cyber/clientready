What we can promise,
and what nobody can.

You're about to spend real money, so here's the part most agencies leave out.

!Month one is mostly building

New sending inboxes need about three weeks of warm-up before cold email can go out safely. Expect a handful of meetings at most in your first month. That's normal, and it's why there's a 3-month minimum.

!Your offer matters

We can put your message in front of the right people, personally, at volume. If the offer itself isn't something buyers want, more emails won't fix that. We'll tell you on day one if we think it needs work.

!We book meetings, you close deals

We aim every meeting at people who match the profile you approve. We can't promise they buy. How you run the call and follow up is what turns a meeting into a client.

!Not every meeting will be perfect

Some people who say yes won't be ready to buy. Anyone who doesn't fit the profile you approved doesn't count toward your numbers, and you can flag them within 48 hours.

Every plan has a clear monthly target.

From month 3, each plan works toward a set number of meetings that actually show up. You see your progress against it every week. If a month comes in under target, we tell you why and what we're changing. We only count meetings with people who match the profile you approved.

    Questions we get every day.

    Will this hurt my company's email or domain?+

    No. We never send from your main domain or your real inbox. We build a separate sending network for your campaigns, warm it up for weeks before any cold email goes out, and watch its health every day. Your everyday email keeps working exactly the way it does now.

    Are the emails really personal, or just my name swapped in?+

    Really personal. Our system researches each company and writes an email around something true and specific about that business, then sends it to a verified decision maker there. You approve the style and the messaging before anything sends, so every email sounds like you.

    Who do the emails go to?+

    Decision makers at companies that match the ideal client you approve: the right industry, size, location and job titles. Owners, partners, founders and department heads. Not random lists, and never consumers.

    Why is there a 3-month minimum?+

    Month one is building and warming up your sending network, so it can't show you real results yet. Month two ramps up, and month three is the first month at full speed. Three months is the shortest honest test of what the system does. After that it's month to month with 30 days' notice.

    What happens right after I pay?+

    You get a Stripe receipt and land on our onboarding page. Fill in the 10-minute form. Within 2 business days you get your private client portal with your target list and sample emails to approve. Building starts right after, and sending starts in week 4.

    How will I know what's happening after I buy?+

    You get a private client portal that always shows which stage you're in: onboarding, building, warming up, live, or full speed. Once emails are sending, it shows your weekly numbers (emails sent, replies, interested replies, meetings booked and meetings that showed up) against your plan's target, and we email you a weekly summary. Need a change? Ask in the portal and it comes straight to us.

    How fast will I see meetings?+

    Expect a handful at most in month one while everything warms up, around half of your plan's range in month two, and your full range from month three.

    What if a month comes in under target?+

    Every plan has a monthly meeting target from month 3, and you can see your progress against it at any time. If a month comes in under target, we show you exactly why and what we're changing, whether that's the list, the emails or the volume. We only count meetings with people who fit the profile you approved and who actually join the call.

    Can I change plans later?+

    Yes. Move up anytime and the new volume starts at your next billing date. Moving down is available once your first 3 months are done.

    What do I actually have to do?+

    About an hour in week one to approve your ideal client and your emails. After that, keep calendar space open and show up to the meetings. We handle the rest.

    Is cold email legal?+

    For business-to-business outreach, yes, when it follows the rules. Every email we send has accurate sender details, an honest subject line and a working opt-out, and we only email business addresses about business topics. If your industry has its own advertising rules, tell us in your onboarding form and we'll write within them.

    Do you work with consumer businesses?+

    No. clientReady is built only for companies that sell to other businesses.
