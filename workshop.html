The 3 Audits

Three industries. Three failure modes. One framework.

Each audit takes ~12 minutes on camera. Real domains, anonymized at company request.

Audit 01 · B2B SaaS

"They're losing $1.4M in pipeline they don't even know exists."

Verdict: Targeting

Demo flow took 19 hours to respond. Industry standard: under 5 minutes. We map the lead-source-to-close funnel and show where every hour of delay costs ~10% conversion.

Audit 02 · Marketing Agency

"Domain reputation 31/100. Torched. The fix takes 14 days."

Verdict: Deliverability

Three prior failed cold-email attempts had nuked their primary domain. We walk through the burner-domain rebuild, SPF/DKIM/DMARC repair, and the 14-day Mailreach warmup that brings it back to 96/100.

Audit 03 · IT Consulting

"41% of revenue depends on one partner. Existential risk."

Verdict: Channel concentration

One rainmaker = a ticking clock. We design the high-trust account-based outbound model that fits a $400/hr consulting engagement , insight-led, not pitch-led, with a 41% conversion on tier-1 prospects.
