Depending on your plan, we find decision makers who match the ideal client you approve, write a personalized email to each one, send them from a sending network we build and manage for you, handle interested replies and book meetings on your calendar. You have 48 hours after a meeting to tell us in writing it was not a fit. The guarantee pauses during any delay on your side, including approvals taking longer than 2 business days, not enough open calendar time, or an overdue payment.
